# About Qarmo

Qarmo is a mobility and delivery platform built for rural Kerala, India. It starts with auto-rickshaw rides and will expand into bike and cab booking and restaurant delivery.

## The two sides of Qarmo

- **Customers** — people who need a ride (and later, food delivered).
- **Partners** — the people who earn on Qarmo by giving rides or making deliveries. A partner is either a **Ride Partner** (auto-rickshaw driver) or a **Delivery Partner** (delivers on a bike, scooter, or bicycle).

Right now, customers and partners share **one single app**. You choose whether you're a Customer or a Partner when you set up your profile. Separate dedicated apps for each may come later, but today it's one app for everyone.

## What makes Qarmo different: the bidding model

Once ride booking is live, Qarmo won't work like a typical fixed-fare taxi app. Instead:

1. A customer requests a ride and sees a **base price** — a fair standard fare for that route, based on distance.
2. Nearby partners can respond by **accepting the base price**, **bidding lower**, or **bidding higher**.
3. The customer sees all the offers as they come in and **picks whichever one they want**.

This bidding flow is part of Qarmo's plan and is **not live yet** — see the roadmap notes for what's currently available versus what's coming.

## Where Qarmo is today

Qarmo is currently in **beta**. The beta app focuses on:

- Getting customers and partners **registered and set up** with a phone number and a profile.
- Giving partners a way to **invite others and earn referral points**.
- Showing customers **where partners are** on a map, so the service feels real and close by.

Ride requests, bidding, deliveries, earnings, and payments are **not part of the beta yet** — they're planned for a later release.

## Who Qarmo is built for

Qarmo's first partners are auto drivers and delivery executives in rural Kerala — many of them are first-time smartphone users on budget Android phones. Because of that, the app is designed to be extremely simple: one thing per screen, big buttons, icons with clear words, and nothing extra. The app currently displays in English; a Malayalam version is prepared and will be enabled in a future release.
