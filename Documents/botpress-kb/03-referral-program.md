# Referral Program (Refer & Earn)

Every Qarmo user gets their own personal **referral code** — a short code you can share with friends.

## How it works

1. **Share your code.** You'll find it on your Home screen (partners) or Account screen. Tap **Share my code** to open your phone's normal share menu with a ready-made message: "Join Qarmo with my code `<CODE>`."
2. **They sign up and finish setup.** The person you invited enters your code in the referral step while setting up their own profile, then completes their profile setup (Finish).
3. **You earn 50 points.** As soon as their profile setup is complete, you're credited **50 points** automatically.

## Where to see your progress

- Partner **Home** screen shows your total points and how many friends have joined ("N friends joined"). If you haven't referred anyone yet, it nudges you to invite a friend.
- **Account** screen also shows your referral code, ready to share again.

## The rules

- Referral is always **optional** — you can finish setting up your account without entering anyone's code.
- A referral code can only be entered **during profile setup**, not added afterward.
- You **can't use your own referral code**.
- If a code doesn't exist, the app shows "Code not found" — you can correct it or just skip the step; it never blocks you from finishing.
- Points are counted as 50 for every friend who has actually finished their own profile setup — someone who started but never finished setting up their profile does not earn you points yet.

## About the points

Points are currently **display-only** — they show your progress and bragging rights, but they can't be redeemed for cash, credit, or anything else yet. Redemption rules are planned for a future release.
