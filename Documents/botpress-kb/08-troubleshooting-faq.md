# Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

**I didn't receive my OTP code. What do I do?**
Wait for the 30-second cooldown, then tap Resend. You can resend up to 3 times per attempt. Double-check you entered the correct phone number, and that your phone has signal. If it still doesn't arrive after 3 resends, wait a while and try starting over.

**It says the OTP is wrong or expired.**
Just re-enter the correct code, or tap Resend to get a fresh one. One wrong attempt doesn't lock your account.

**My photo or document upload failed.**
This usually happens with a weak connection or a very large file. The app shows "Upload failed. Try again" — nothing else you entered is lost, so just retry that step. Make sure you have a stable internet connection.

**The app asked for camera/photo/location permission and I said no by mistake.**
You can turn the permission back on from your phone's Settings for the Qarmo app (the app also offers an "Open settings" shortcut when this happens). Nothing else in the app is blocked while you do this, except the specific feature that needs the permission (adding a photo, or showing you on the map).

**I entered a referral code and it says "Code not found."**
Double-check the code for typos — it's usually 6 characters. If you're sure it's right and it still doesn't work, you can simply skip the referral step; entering a code is always optional and never blocks you from finishing setup.

**Can I use my own referral code?**
No — self-referrals aren't allowed.

**I closed the app in the middle of setting up my profile. Did I lose my progress?**
No. The next time you open the app and verify your phone, you'll be taken back to finish the profile setup, picking up from where you left off.

**I tapped Back while setting up my profile and it signed me out.**
That's expected. Since you're already phone-verified by that point, going "back" isn't possible without leaving the session — the app asks you to confirm first and explains you'll need to verify your phone again to continue.

**The map isn't showing any partners near me.**
That area may simply not have partners registered yet, or their pins may be just outside your current view — try panning or zooming out. If you've denied location access, the map defaults to Kochi rather than your actual area.

**Why can't I see ride requests, deliveries, or earnings?**
Those features aren't live yet — the beta currently focuses on getting people registered, connecting through referrals, and showing partners on the map. See the roadmap notes for what's coming next.

**How do I change my phone number?**
You can't change it from inside the app in the beta — your phone number is fixed to your account once verified.

**How do I log out?**
Go to the Account tab and tap Log out, then confirm.
