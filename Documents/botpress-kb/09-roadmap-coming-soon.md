# What's Coming Soon (Not Live Yet)

Qarmo is currently in **beta**, focused on registration, referrals, and showing partners on a map. The following are part of the plan but **are not available yet** — if asked about any of these, make clear they're coming in a future release, not something you can do today.

- **Booking a ride.** Customers requesting an auto (and later bike/cab) ride.
- **The bidding flow.** Partners accepting a ride's base price or bidding lower/higher, and customers picking an offer.
- **Delivery jobs.** Delivery Partners receiving and fulfilling restaurant/package delivery requests.
- **Job Board.** The partner tab that will eventually list ride and delivery job opportunities.
- **Earnings.** Any in-app tracking of money earned from rides or deliveries.
- **Payments.** All payment and payout handling.
- **Redeeming referral points.** Points are currently for display and progress only — they can't yet be converted into cash, credit, or rewards.
- **Document verification.** Aadhaar and driving licence uploads are stored but not yet reviewed or approved by anyone.
- **Bike and cab booking.** Phase 1 covers auto rides only.
- **Restaurant / food delivery** ordering, and the separate restaurant-owner app.
- **Live GPS tracking** of an ongoing ride or delivery. Partner positions on the map today are a single snapshot taken when they last opened the app, not real-time tracking.
- **Separate customer and partner apps.** Today, everyone uses one shared app; dedicated apps for each may come later.

For everything that *is* available right now — registration, referrals, the partner/customer maps, and account management — see the other Qarmo knowledge base articles.
