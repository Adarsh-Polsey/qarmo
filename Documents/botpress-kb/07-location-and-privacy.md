# Location and Privacy

Qarmo uses location differently for customers and for partners.

## For customers

- Your location is used **only** to center the map on your Home screen when you open it.
- It is **never saved** to Qarmo's systems and **never shown** to partners or anyone else.
- If you don't allow location access, your map simply opens centered on Kochi instead. You can still pan around and see partners in other areas — nothing else in the app is affected.

## For partners

- Each time you open the app, Qarmo captures your location **once** and saves it as your "last known" position, so customers can see roughly where partners are nearby.
- This is a **single snapshot on open** — Qarmo does not track your location continuously or in the background while you use the app.
- If you don't allow location access, you simply won't appear on the customer map. Everything else about the app still works normally; you're never blocked from using it.

## What customers actually see

A partner's position on the customer map is shown only as a small type icon (🛺 or 🛵) — no name, phone number, or photo. There's nothing to tap on the pin either. It's a general "partners are active here" signal, not a way to identify or contact a specific person through the map.

## A note on freshness

Because partner locations are only updated when that partner opens the app, a pin can reflect where someone was the last time they opened Qarmo, not their real-time position. There's no live tracking or moving pins in the beta.
