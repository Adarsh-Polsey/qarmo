# Registration and Login

Qarmo has **one single entry flow** for everyone — there's no separate "Register" button and "Log in" button to choose between. Everyone starts the same way, and the app works out whether you're a new user or a returning one.

## Step 1 — Welcome screen

The first screen you see says "Welcome to Qarmo — Rides & deliveries, made simple." Tap **Get started** to continue.

## Step 2 — Phone number and OTP

- Enter your phone number.
- Qarmo sends a 6-digit one-time code (OTP) by SMS.
- Enter the code to verify.
- There is **no password** — your phone number and the OTP are all you need to sign in, every time.

**Didn't get the code?** You can request it again ("Resend") after a 30-second wait. You can resend up to 3 times per attempt. If you still don't receive it after that, double-check the phone number you entered is correct and that your phone has signal.

**Wrong or expired code?** The app shows a plain error message and lets you try again — it won't lock you out for one mistake.

## Step 3 — What happens after your number is verified

- **First time on this phone number:** you land on the profile setup screen (see below) to finish creating your account.
- **Returning user with a completed profile:** you go straight into the app — no extra steps.
- **Returning user who quit partway through setup last time:** you're taken right back to finish setting up your profile, picking up where you left off (nothing you already entered is lost).

## Setting up your profile

On the profile setup screen you first choose **Customer** or **Partner**.

### Customer setup

The customer path is short: enter your **full name**, then tap **Finish**. That's it — you're immediately using the app. No documents, no extra steps.

### Partner setup

1. Choose whether you're a **Ride Partner** (auto-rickshaw) or a **Delivery Partner** (bike, scooter, or bicycle).
2. Fill in your **name** and your **city / area**.
3. Enter your **vehicle number** (e.g. `KL 07 BZ 1234` — standard Indian plate format).
4. Add a **profile photo** (camera or gallery).
5. Upload a photo of your **Aadhaar card**.
6. Upload a photo of your **driving licence**.
7. Optionally, enter a **referral code** from someone who invited you — or skip this step. An unrecognized code shows "Code not found" so you can fix it, but it never stops you from finishing.
8. Tap **Finish**.

As soon as you finish, your account is complete and **you can use the app right away** — there is no waiting for approval before you can start exploring the app (see the document-verification notes for more on this).

## Going back during setup

Because you're already phone-verified by the time you reach the profile setup screen, tapping **Back** there will sign you out and ask you to confirm first — it warns that you'll need to verify your phone number again to continue. This is intentional: there's no way to go "back" to an earlier screen without leaving the verified session.

## Staying logged in

Once you're set up, Qarmo keeps you signed in. Opening the app again later takes you straight to your home screen — you don't need to log in every time.

## Logging out

Go to the **Account** tab and tap **Log out**. You'll be asked "Log out?" with a Yes/No confirmation before it happens.
