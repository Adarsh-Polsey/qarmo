# Account and Profile

Your **Account** tab shows your profile and lets you make a few changes.

## What you can see

- Your photo, name, and phone number.
- Your account type (Customer or Partner).
- For partners: your partner type (Ride/Delivery), city, vehicle number, referral code, and the status of your submitted documents (Aadhaar card and driving licence).
- App version and the date you joined ("Member since").

## What you can change

- **Name** — tap it to edit and save a new one.
- **Profile photo** — tap your photo (or the camera badge on it) to take a new one or choose from your gallery.

## What you can't change in the app

- **Phone number** — once verified, it's fixed. Changing the number tied to your account isn't something you can do from inside the app in the beta.
- **Documents** (Aadhaar card, driving licence) — these are uploaded once during profile setup and can't be replaced from the Account screen in the beta.

## Sharing your referral code

Your referral code is shown on the Account screen too — tap it to share it again any time, using the same "Join Qarmo with my code `<CODE>`" message.

## Logging out

Tap **Log out** at the bottom of the Account screen. You'll be asked to confirm ("Log out?" Yes/No) before you're signed out.
