# Getting Around the App

After you finish setting up your profile, the app shows three tabs at the bottom of the screen. Which three tabs you see depends on whether you're a **Customer** or a **Partner**.

## Customer tabs

- **Home** — a full-screen map centered on your location, showing nearby partners.
- **AI Agent** — this chat assistant, where you can ask questions about Qarmo.
- **Account** — your profile details and log out.

## Partner tabs

- **Home** — an ID-card style summary of you: photo, name, partner type, city, vehicle number, your referral points, and your referral code.
- **Job Board** — where ride/delivery job listings will appear. This is **coming soon** and not live yet in the beta.
- **Account** — your profile details and log out.

## Customer Home (the map)

- The map opens centered on your current location.
- Nearby partners show up as simple icons: 🛺 for Ride Partners, 🛵 for Delivery Partners. There's no name, phone number, or extra info shown — just the icon, so you can see partners are active in the area.
- Pan or zoom the map to look at another town or area — it automatically loads the partners there after you stop moving the map.
- If an area has a lot of partners, the map shows the nearest 100 and a "Zoom in to see more" hint.
- If you haven't allowed location access, the map opens centered on Kochi instead, with a banner suggesting you turn location on. You can still use the map either way.

## Partner Home (the ID card)

Your Home screen shows:

- Your photo, name, partner type (Ride/Delivery), city, and vehicle number, laid out like an ID card.
- Your total **referral points** in green, and how many friends have joined through your code.
- Your **referral code**, with a **Share my code** button.

Ride requests, deliveries, and earnings are **not shown here yet** — that part of the app is still coming.

## The "partners on Qarmo" counter

Both Home screens show a live running total at the top — something like "512 partners on Qarmo." This number is the same for everyone and updates automatically as new partners join, without needing to refresh the screen. It's there so everyone can see the platform is real and growing.
