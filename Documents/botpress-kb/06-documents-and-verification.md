# Documents and Verification

Partners (both Ride Partners and Delivery Partners) upload two documents while setting up their profile:

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Driving licence**

Each one is captured with the camera or picked from the photo gallery.

## Important: documents are not verified yet

In the current beta, these documents are **collected and stored safely, but nobody reviews, checks, approves, or rejects them yet**. Because of this:

- No partner is ever labeled "unverified" anywhere in the app.
- Nothing about your document status blocks you from using the app.
- As soon as you finish setting up your profile, **you can use the app immediately** — there is no waiting period or approval step in the beta.

Document review and approval are planned for a later release, once the platform is ready for live ride and delivery requests.

## If a document upload fails

If an upload doesn't go through (for example, due to a weak connection or the file being too large), the app shows a plain "Upload failed. Try again" message. Nothing you've already filled in earlier in the setup is lost — you just retry that one step.
